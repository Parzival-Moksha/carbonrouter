CARBONROUTER — THE HUMAN ROUTING LAYER
=======================================

INTRO / PITCH (v0.1.0 landing page):

To meet the right person at the right time could be life-changing. What if once-in-a-decade encounters could happen every week? Can AI improve on our current human connection stack of happenstance, word-of-mouth, plus LinkedIn?

How many tokens does it take for an LLM to generate a high-resolution map of what makes you productive? Could we compute the highest expected synergy for sets of human interactions across hundreds of profiles in one context?

Frontier LLMs can juggle rich preference manifolds of hundreds of people in their context, enabling high-dimensional instant sorting. SOTA LLMs with quality context engineering are the approximation algorithm killers. NP-hard no more.

Opus scores your productivity, synergy, and complementarity across dozens of dimensions with SOUL.md's of other, pre-selected users and computes your highest scoring ideal monthly routing schedule.

The more data about you, the higher the routing resolution. Want your personal agent to handle your profile creation? Or do you have 10 minutes to talk to our curious agent per text or voice? Wanna do both? Let us get who you are and we'll email your ideal routing for next month.

This is alpha, so just do a quick interview and recommend to 2 friends who you think would benefit from CarbonRouter. You'll hear from us when we know where to route you.

1.0 will give you great recommendations. v2.0 will route you better than you could. v3.0 will be your exocortex's motor neurons to his meat puppet.

SYNERGY CRITERIA:
- Productivity synergy: would working together make both more productive?
- Complementarity: do their skills/personality gaps fill each other?
- Similarity: shared values, interests, wavelength
- Learning potential: what could they teach each other?
- Novelty: how different are their worlds? serendipity factor
- Collaboration potential: concrete project ideas they could build together
- Network value: who do they know that the other needs?

PRIVACY TIERS:
1. Full anonymous — user sees only route-together proposal, no identity revealed to any user
2. Mutual reveal — if both users accept a route-together proposal, contacts reveal
3. Open — contact visible to all users with a route-together proposal (above a certain synergy score)

POST-INTERVIEW FLOW:
1. Interview ends (resolution >= 0.85 or turn >= 12 or user clicks finish)
2. Character sheet generated and displayed
3. Contact collection: "Where should we reach you when we find your routes?"
4. Consent: "May we include you in other users' routing computations?"
5. Save & download - i mean a save button first, and when its saved, we can then display a not so super prominent and flashy download button.
